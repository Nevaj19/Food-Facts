A Field Guide to Your Food

Food Lens

Search any product to see how processed it is, how healthy it is, and exactly what's inside.

⌕
01

Nutri-Score

A graded letter from A to E that summarises overall nutritional quality — calories, sugar, salt, fat against fibre and protein.

02

NOVA Group

A 1–4 scale measuring how processed a food is. 1 means whole or minimally processed; 4 means ultra-processed.

03

Eco-Score

An environmental rating from A to E covering packaging, transport, farming methods, and carbon footprint.

Data from Open Food Facts — a free, open, collaborative database of food products from around the world.
